MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (VENDOR ENGAGEMENT)

This Agreement is entered into in connection with the provision of services between the parties.

1. Confidential Information Defined
"Confidential Information" means information disclosed by either party relating to its business operations, systems, pricing, personnel, or customers, whether or not marked confidential.

2. Exclusions
This Agreement imposes no obligation with respect to information that (a) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information; (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided prompt notice is given.

3. Obligations
Each party shall hold the other's Confidential Information in confidence and use it only as necessary to perform or receive the services.

4. Term of Confidentiality
The obligations under this Agreement shall continue for five (5) years from the date of disclosure.

5. Return of Confidential Information
Upon expiry or written request, each party shall return or destroy Confidential Information within sixty (60) days.

6. Injunctive Relief
Each party may seek injunctive relief to prevent unauthorised use or disclosure.

7. Limitation of Liability
Each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid under the related services agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

8. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas, and the parties consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Travis County, Texas.

9. Assignment
Either party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets, upon written notice.

10. Severability
If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.
