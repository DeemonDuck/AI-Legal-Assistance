CONFIDENTIALITY AND NON-SOLICITATION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between the Company and the undersigned contractor in connection with services to be provided to the Company.

1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information of the Company, including its methods, client information, pricing, and personnel data, disclosed to the Contractor in the course of the engagement.

2. Exclusions
The Contractor's obligations do not extend to information that (a) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information; (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided prompt notice is given.

3. Obligations of the Contractor
The Contractor shall not use or disclose Confidential Information except as required to perform the services, and shall not retain copies after the engagement ends.

4. Term
The Contractor's confidentiality obligations shall continue for two (2) years following the conclusion of the engagement.

5. Non-Solicitation
For a period of twelve (12) months following the conclusion of the engagement, the Contractor shall not solicit for employment any employee of the Company with whom the Contractor worked directly.

6. Return of Property
On conclusion of the engagement, the Contractor shall return all Company property and materials within fourteen (14) days.

7. Remedies
The Company may seek injunctive relief in the event of any breach or threatened breach.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois, and the parties consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of Cook County, Illinois.

9. Assignment
The Contractor may not assign this Agreement. The Company may assign it to a successor entity.

10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding of the parties on its subject matter.
