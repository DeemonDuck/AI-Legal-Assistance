MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (PARTNERSHIP)

The parties intend to explore a joint commercial partnership and will exchange confidential information in doing so.

1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public business, technical, and commercial information disclosed by either party in connection with the proposed partnership.

2. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that (a) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of the Confidential Information; (d) is rightfully received from a third party without restriction; or (e) is required to be disclosed by law or court order, provided prompt notice is given.

3. Obligations
Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it applies to its own, and shall disclose it only to personnel with a need to know.

4. Term
The obligations under this Agreement continue for three (3) years from the date of each disclosure.

5. Non-Solicitation
During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months thereafter, neither party shall solicit for employment any employee of the other party introduced during the discussions.

6. Return or Destruction
Each party shall return or destroy the other's Confidential Information within thirty (30) days of written request.

7. Remedies
Each party may seek injunctive relief in respect of a breach or threatened breach.

8. Governing Law and Venue
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, and the parties consent to the jurisdiction of the courts of King County, Washington.

9. Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement supersedes all prior understandings relating to its subject matter.
