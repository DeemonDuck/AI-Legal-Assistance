CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (INVESTOR DILIGENCE)

The Company wishes to disclose certain information to the Recipient for the purpose of evaluating a potential investment.

1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means non-public information provided by the Company concerning its business, financial condition, technology, and prospects.

2. Exceptions
The Recipient's obligations do not apply to information that (a) was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure; (b) is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party; (c) is independently developed by the Receiving Party; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or court order.

3. Obligations of Recipient
The Recipient shall keep the Confidential Information confidential and use it solely to evaluate the potential investment, and may share it with its professional advisers who are bound by equivalent obligations.

4. Term
The obligations of the Recipient shall continue for two (2) years from the date of disclosure.

5. Return of Information
On written request the Recipient shall destroy the Confidential Information and confirm destruction, subject to retention in routine electronic backups.

6. Remedies
The Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in respect of any breach.

7. No Warranty
The Confidential Information is provided without warranty as to accuracy or completeness.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

9. Dispute Resolution
Disputes arising under this Agreement shall be settled by binding arbitration in New York, New York.

10. Entire Agreement
This Agreement represents the entire agreement of the parties concerning its subject matter.
